ENGR 112 · SPRING 2026

HAZBOT

Autonomous hazard-detecting robot

A four-person team build. HAZBOT drives a hazard course on its own, reads its sensors in real time, and reacts at each zone with lights, a buzzer, and turn signals. The hardest part for me was the Arduino code: getting sensors and motors to behave on real hardware.

  1. Obstacle Blue LED past 1 ft, red LED and buzzer inside 1 ft, then stop and turn 90°
  2. Precision travel Drive exactly 5 ft, then turn 90° right
  3. Heat zone Above 35 °C, blink red and speed through
  4. Voice command Mic reading above 600 of 1023 stops the bot and turns it
  5. VOC evacuation Air quality index of 3 or higher sends it back toward the finish
  6. Finish Stop within 15 cm of the wall, green LED
Controller
SparkFun RedBoard (Arduino Uno compatible)
Sensors
HC-SR04 ultrasonic, ENS160 + BME280 over I2C, analog mic
Drive
2 DC motors on a TB6612FNG driver
Build
SolidWorks parametric parts, 3D-printed mounts
Fixes
Startup false zeros, left/right motor drift, sensor warm-up
